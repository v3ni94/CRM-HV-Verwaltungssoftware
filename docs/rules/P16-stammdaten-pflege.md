Migration 0265 |
| Source status | Fachliche Umsetzung and Produktschutz. No legal rule is introduced: no limit of a deposit, no notice period, no mandate expiry rule beyond the explicit `valid_until` of the mandate (the 36 month rule of the SEPA scheme is not implemented, no verified source in annex C). Amount, period and type of a payment row or payment plan are locked as soon as a posted receivable item (`status = posted`) refers to the row (rule 7 and B08, correction by new row and reversal); `revenue_account_id` and `document_id` stay changeable. Bank account: IBAN, kind and legal entity are fixed (6.9.1, D56), ending sets `valid_to` and removes the default flag |
| Acceptance case | None in annex D. | Implementation | Deposit status model: `open` to `active` or `settled`, `active` to `open` or `settled`, `settled` final (only documents can still be added); amount and instalments only before the first deposit movement. Party deletion only without references (database restriction is translated to 409). Tag merge moves links without duplicates. `mark_mandate_used` sets `last_used_at` and moves `first` to `recurring`; it is called by the collection once G2 is open and by nothing else.
