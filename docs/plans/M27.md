## M27-01 pricing structure (decided 24.09.2026)

* Price per unit, module and month with optional minimum (`license.min_monthly_amount`,
  migration 0029). `GET /platform/tenants/{id}/billing-preview?month=` computes net amounts per
  module from the usage counter (a licence counts if valid on any day of the month) and flags
  units over quota. Amounts, VAT and invoicing of licence fees stay open (M27-01).
