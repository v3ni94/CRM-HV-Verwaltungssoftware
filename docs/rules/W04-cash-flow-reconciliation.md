# Jahresabrechnung als nachvollziehbare Überleitung (7.8, W04)

| ID | W04 |
| Title | Jahresabrechnung als nachvollziehbare Überleitung |
| Scope | WEG annual statement (M24): Gesamtgeldfluss of the calendar year (bank and cash accounts of the community's ledger) against the distribution relevant costs of the individual statements. Transfers between own bank and cash accounts are neither inflow nor outflow. Takeover year (A80): the posted opening balance entries of the statement year are read as the migration journal of the ledger (6.9.10), see the row Takeover year below |
| Source status | annex C R04 (§ 28 WEG) for the calendar year and the resolution over the statement; annex C P02 (BGH V ZR 251/10, cash flow and heating cost bridge) is a source gap: the official full text was not verified, so the structure of the bridge is a functional requirement of the master prompt (W04), not a claimed legal form. Which explanation codes are legally sufficient is not decided (M24-03) |
| Acceptance case | no annex D case of its own; `tests/integration/test_m24_loans.py::test_w04_reconciliation_blocks_until_explained` with hand computed values (docstring of the test file); the existing D01, D18, D19, D54 and W12 fixtures now book their costs so that their reconciliation closes |
| Implementation | `mhvp.hoa.calc.cash_flow_reconciliation`: opening and closing balance per bank and cash account, inflows and outflows by counter account category (cost, creditor, debtor, loan, reserve, revenue, other) and by W10 loan item kind; bridge from `outflows` over `cost_paid` (less reserve, loan positions, owner refunds, transit, refunds on costs) and `creditor_timing` (creditor payments minus costs booked through creditors in the year) to `cost_booked`, plus the manager's explained differences (`hoa_statement.reconciliation_notes`, codes `heating_accrual`, `creditor_timing`, `prior_year`, `other`, each with amount and reason) to `cost_distributed`; `unexplained` is the rest. `mhvp.hoa.package.blocking_checks` finding `reconciliation_unexplained` (and `cash_check` when opening plus flows do not give the closing balance) refuses the internal approval (409, W12). `PUT /api/v1/hoa/statements/{id}/reconciliation-notes` only in status draft or calculated. Snapshot key `reconciliation` at calculation, live block in the package. Migration 0116 |
| Takeover year (A80, 6.9.10) | Posted entries of kind `opening_balance` within the statement year are the takeover from the old system (the migrated journal tables `migrated_journal_entry` and `migrated_journal_line` of 6.9.10 are not built yet, M8/M10; until then the reviewed opening balance entries with their source carry the takeover). Their cash lines form the opening balance of bank and cash (`cash.accounts[].opening_migration`, `cash.opening` = prior year balance + migration) instead of the prior year balance; they are neither inflow nor outflow, so `check_ok` still holds. Their cost lines (costs of the pre period carried over from the old system) are the explained difference `migration_opening` in the bridge (automatic row with `migration: true`), so `unexplained` = distributed minus booked on the platform minus manual notes minus migration costs plus structure residual. Block `migration` (applied, entries, sources, cutoff of the ledger, cash_opening, cost_opening, other_opening, note) in the snapshot and the package; CRM shows "Übernahme aus Altsystem" on the statement page. `migration_opening` is also a manual note code for a takeover that was not carried as cost lines. Opening balance is a balance sheet fact, never an expense: the cost lines of the opening entry are the pre period costs, not costs of the platform year. Hand computed case `test_w04_reconciliation_migration_year` (bank 5.000,00 and costs 800,00 from the old system, 600,00 booked, 1.400,00 distributed, unexplained 0,00, releasable without a manual note) |
