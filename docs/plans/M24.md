# M24 WEG Wirtschaftsplan und Abrechnung: plan and result

Status: implemented behind G4 (2026-09-23). Source: MASTER-PROMPT 6.9.3, 7.3, W01 to W08,
annex D (D01 to D03), 18 (M24).

* `mhvp.hoa` (models, calc, routers `/hoa`), migration 0022: resolutions and collection,
  economic plan (items, calculation per unit with monthly rounding difference, apply only
  after a binding resolution), statement (cost items, calculation of result against resolved
  advances, arrears separate, reserve actual closing, asset report), status model with four
  eyes approval and resolution bound to the snapshot hash, issuing and posting need G4,
  posting books only the result per unit, new version keeps the resolved one unchanged.
* Test: `tests/integration/test_m24_hoa.py` (D01, D02, D03, W06 binding, G4, plan rounding).
* Open: M24-01, M24-02, A-034. D04 (internal transfer) is covered by banking (M11).

## Extension W09, W12 (2026-09-24)

* `mhvp.hoa.levies`, migration 0025: special levies (purpose, total, key, affected units,
  instalments, resolution on the snapshot, application as monthly contract payments of type
  special_levy, earmarked funds report). Open: W09-01, A-038.
* `mhvp.hoa.package`: statement package (cost items with key and split, units, reserve,
  asset report, plan, resolution, audit reports) and blocking checks; the internal approval of
  a HOA statement is refused while a finding exists (missing unit, owner gap or overlap, cost
  item without account, unresolved bank transactions of the year).
* Tests: `tests/integration/test_w09_special_levy.py`, package check in `test_m24_hoa.py`.
* W09-01 (decided 24.09.2026, migration 0028): amendment creates a new version bound to a new
  resolution; per unit difference as a one month charge or as a draft credit entry (no
  automatic posting or payout); the old version becomes superseded; the report covers the
  instalment months of the first version and every difference month.

## Gap list A18 to A20 (2026-09-26): D18, D19, D20, D54

* D18 (W03): `mhvp.hoa.package.blocking_checks` finding `scope_unfounded` when a cost item's key
  reaches only part of the community's units (sub community, entrance, user group) and the
  basis text names neither a resolution nor the Teilungserklärung or Gemeinschaftsordnung as
  source. The package shows the note, the internal approval is refused (409, W12). Product
  protection: term check on the free text; a structured source link (document or resolution
  on the cost item) would need a migration and stays open (A18).
* D19 (W08): the reserve block of the statement snapshot shows `bank_balance` of the reserve
  bank accounts (kind reserve or default account 001201), `bank_difference` against the
  accounting closing and a note; the calculation writes no entry.
* D20 (W09): test of instalments with a later partial refund by amendment: purpose, Soll/Ist,
  use, due months and reason stay, credits remain drafts, use of funds is not counted twice.
* D54: `patch_resolution` records a contest and changes nothing else; the package shows the
  validity state and the follow up steps apart from the status; `post_statement` refuses with
  an explicit D54 message while the resolution is not positive, final or legally binding;
  an already posted result is never reversed by a status change.
* Tests: `test_m24_hoa.py::test_d18_sub_community_without_basis_blocks_release`,
  `::test_d19_reserve_contribution_unpaid_no_settlement_entry`,
  `::test_d54_contested_resolution_blocks_posting_and_reverses_nothing`,
  `test_w09_special_levy.py::test_d20_levy_instalments_with_partial_refund`.

## Gap list A59, A60 (2026-09-26): W10 and W04

* A59 (W10): `mhvp.hoa.finance` and migration 0116 (tables `hoa_measure`, `hoa_measure_financing`,
  `hoa_loan`, `hoa_loan_item`, `hoa_insurance_claim`, `hoa_insurance_claim_item`, all with RLS).
  Loan: lender, principal, rate, term, instalment, loan account; items disbursement, repayment,
  interest and fee, each with an optional `journal_entry_id` of the same ledger. Only items
  with a posted entry count (`booked`); the loan balance is derived from them and shown next
  to the loan account balance (difference explained, never settled). Claim: insurer, policy,
  claim number, deductible, recourse party; items damage_cost, benefit, deductible, regress,
  owner_payment (only with the ownership contract); net burden from booked items. Measure:
  kind (maintenance, structural change, undecided) only with `kind_basis` (facts and legal
  basis, W10), cost frame, resolution, financing shares from reserve, special levy, loan or
  other. Documents through `DocumentLink` (entity types `hoa_loan`, `hoa_insurance_claim`,
  `hoa_measure`). CRM: sections on `/weg/[propertyId]` and pages `darlehen/[loanId]`,
  `versicherung/[claimId]`, `massnahme/[measureId]` (`FinanceForms.tsx`). Permissions:
  accounting:read and accounting:create as in the rest of the module (no hoa:* permission
  exists in `mhvp.core.auth.permissions`).
* A60 (W04): `calc.cash_flow_reconciliation` (rule file `docs/rules/W04-cash-flow-reconciliation.md`):
  opening and closing balance per bank and cash account, inflows and outflows by counter
  account category and by loan item kind, bridge outflows -> cost paid -> cost booked
  (creditor timing) -> cost distributed with the manager's explained differences
  (`hoa_statement.reconciliation_notes`, `PUT /hoa/statements/{id}/reconciliation-notes`,
  codes heating_accrual, creditor_timing, prior_year, other). The snapshot carries the
  reconciliation, the package shows it live; `blocking_checks` adds `reconciliation_unexplained`
  (and `cash_check`) and the internal approval is refused. CRM: block on the statement page
  with the notes form (draft or calculated only).
* Tests: `tests/integration/test_m24_loans.py` (3 tests, hand computed values in the module
  docstring), `apps/web-crm/src/components/hoa/FinanceForms.test.tsx` (4 tests). The D01, D18,
  D19, D54 and W12 fixtures now book their costs (helper `_book_cost`), the W12 test expects
  the additional finding for an unbooked cost item, D01 counts 7 entries in the audit population.
* Open: M24-03 (interest and repayment in the statement, explanation codes, tax treatment of
  claims), migration year of the reconciliation (6.9.10), structured resolution link on claims.
