| ID | W10 |
| Title | Darlehen, Versicherungen, größere Maßnahmen |
| Scope | WEG module (M24): loans, insurance claims and larger measures of a community with an own ledger (W01). Recording and evidence only; no posting, no payment, no allocation into the statement by this module |
| Source status | annex C R02 (§ 16, § 21 WEG) for the decision competence and the distinction maintenance versus structural change (recorded as facts and legal basis on the measure, never derived from an account proposal alone); the separation of disbursement, repayment, interest and fees and the traceable linking of benefit, damage cost, deductible, recourse and owner payments are functional requirements of the master prompt (W10). Tax and allocation treatment per case, and the distribution of interest and repayment in the annual statement, are not decided (M24-03, release before G4) |
| Acceptance case | no annex D case of its own; `tests/integration/test_m24_loans.py::test_w10_loan_measure_items_and_separation` (loan 20.000,00, posted disbursement and repayment, planned interest and fee, balance 19.000,00 against the loan account, document link, read only role, foreign tenant 404) and `::test_w10_insurance_claim_items` (damage 4.000,00, benefit 3.500,00, net burden 500,00, owner payment only with the ownership contract) |
| Implementation | `mhvp.hoa.models`: `hoa_measure` (kind, kind_basis, cost frame, resolution, status), `hoa_measure_financing` (reserve, special levy, loan, other), `hoa_loan` (lender, principal, rate, term, instalment, loan account), `hoa_loan_item` (disbursement, repayment, interest, fee with `journal_entry_id`), `hoa_insurance_claim` (insurer, claim number, deductible, recourse party, status), `hoa_insurance_claim_item` (damage_cost, benefit, deductible, regress, owner_payment with contract). `mhvp.hoa.finance` endpoints under `/api/v1/hoa/{measures,loans,insurance-claims}` (accounting:read and accounting:create like the other WEG endpoints; no separate hoa:* permission exists in the catalogue). Amounts count only with a posted journal entry of the same ledger (`booked`); balances are derived from booked items and compared with the loan account, never settled. Documents via `DocumentLink` with entity types `hoa_loan`, `hoa_insurance_claim`, `hoa_measure`. Loan items feed the W04 reconciliation as loan positions. Migration 0116, RLS on all six tables |
| Change reason | A59 of the gap list 26.09.2026 (only account defaults existed). A78 and A79 (26.09.2026): instalment schedule and structured resolution link on the claim, see below. Open: M24-03 (interest and repayment in the statement) |
| Instalment schedule (A78) | `mhvp.hoa.calc.loan_schedule`: annuity plan when an instalment is set (constant instalment, interest share falls, repayment share rises), linear plan when only the term is set (constant repayment principal / term, last instalment takes the rounding rest). Monthly interest = open balance * rate / 100 / 12, rounded half up to the cent per instalment (Decimal, no float); first instalment one month after the start, the last one closes the balance exactly; with instalment and term the plan stops after the term and reports the remaining balance as `residual`. Neither instalment nor term, or an instalment below the first month's interest: 422. Day count conventions, fees, tranches and rate changes are not modelled. `GET /api/v1/hoa/loans/{id}/schedule` returns the rows, totals and `comparison` (per month: planned against booked repayment and interest items, difference booked minus planned; `calc.schedule_comparison`). Display only, nothing is posted; `note_text` "Orientierung, maßgeblich ist der Darlehensvertrag." Hand computed values in the docstring of `tests/integration/test_m24_loans.py` (`test_w10_loan_schedule_hand_values`, schedule block in `test_w10_loan_measure_items_and_separation`). CRM: block Ratenplan with comparison on `/weg/[propertyId]/darlehen/[loanId]` |
| Resolution link on the claim (A79) | `hoa_insurance_claim.resolution_id` (migration 0128), on create and `PATCH` checked by `finance._check_resolution` against the resolutions of the same community (422 otherwise); `PATCH` accepts status, claim number or resolution alone. CRM: selection from the community's resolutions in the claim form and on `/weg/[propertyId]/versicherung/[claimId]` (`ResolutionSelect`); shown as number, date and subject |
