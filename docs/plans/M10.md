# M10 Buchungskreis: plan and result

Status: implemented, not accepted (2026-09-23). Source: MASTER-PROMPT 6.4, 6.9.1, 6.9.8,
6.9.10, 6.9.13, 7.1 to 7.3, 18 (M10).
Acceptance: B01 to B09 and relevant D tests passed; legal entity and WEG/SEV separation shown;
history, concurrency, abort and retry checked; G1 documented. G1 itself stays closed.

## Decisions

* Package `mhvp.accounting`, migration 0010 (RLS on all tables, database guards as triggers).
* One ledger per legal entity (unique). Accounts from a tenant template; the annex A.1 draft
  template is created unreleased (V8). Debtor accounts adopt the numbers reserved in M5.
* Entries: draft → posted. Posting assigns the gapless number (ADR 0007), creates open items
  (debit on debtor, credit on creditor for claim kinds) and applies an explicit settlement plan
  (no automatic repayment order, M10-03).
* Reversal instead of change; reversal of a settling entry adds negative settlements dated at
  the reversal (history stays true as of earlier dates). A claim with settled payments cannot
  be reversed before those payments.
* Locking (`locked_until`) only forward; reversals default to the first open day.
* Opening balances need approval by a second person before posting.
* Parallel operation: ledgers are not leading (`leading_system = immoware24`). Switching to
  the platform as leading system requires G1 (checked, closed by default).
* Reports: journal, account sheet, trial balance as of a date, open items as of a date,
  consistency checks.
* Permission resource `accounting`; existing tenants receive the new role permissions when
  `make seed` runs again (idempotent `ensure_system_roles`).
