Status: implemented; majority rules per community and minutes template open (2026-09-23).
Source: MASTER-PROMPT 6.9.12, PÜ06 to PÜ13, R03, R05, 18 (M25).

## M25-01 rule set (decided 24.09.2026)

* `majority_rule` (migration 0027) per community with principle, share of votes cast
  (strictly greater or at least), optional minimum share of all MEA, unanimity, mandatory
  source. Agenda items may reference a rule; the tally evaluates it and shows each check.
  The announcement stays with the chair and must match the tally.
* Test: rule block in `tests/integration/test_m25_meeting.py` (test values, no legal claim).

## M25-01 subject rules (26.09.2026)

* `hoa_majority_rule` (migration 0114): rule per subject kind, tenant default with optional
  override per GdWE, majority type, counting basis, mandatory source, release by a second
  person. `resolution.subject_kind` and `resolution.majority_check` (protocol note, no status
  change). Endpoints under `/hoa/majority-rules/subject-rules` and
  `/hoa/resolutions/{id}/majority-check`. Details: `docs/rules/M25-01-mehrheitsregeln.md`.
* Test: `tests/unit/test_m25_majority_rules.py` (test values, no legal claim).
