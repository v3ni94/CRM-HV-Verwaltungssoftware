# M26 Mieterhöhung und Vermietung: plan and result

Status: process implemented; OpenImmo export (M26-02) implemented 25.09.2026, read only;
legal checks and energy certificate completeness open (2026-09-25). Source: MASTER-PROMPT
6.3 (rent_increase_case), 18 (M26).
Acceptance "Mieterhöhungsprozess durchgängig": draft, check, four eyes approval, sending
(G3 plus documented legal review), consent with evidence, application to the rent history.

* `mhvp.letting` (models, routers `/letting`, tasks), migration 0023 (rent_increase_case,
  prospect): arithmetic check against entered cap and comparison rent with source, block
  date, vacancy list, exposé draft from master data, prospects with mandatory deletion date
  and daily purge.
* OpenImmo 1.2.7 export (M26-02, docs/rules/M26-02.md, 25.09.2026): `mhvp.letting.openimmo`
  (`GET /letting/listings/{listing_id}/openimmo.xml`,
  `GET /letting/listings/{listing_id}/openimmo-check`,
  `GET /letting/listings/openimmo.zip`), read only, no FLOWFACT or other portal call. CRM
  button "OpenImmo exportieren" on the listing detail page shows the German check result
  before offering the download. No official OpenImmo 1.2.7 XSD is bundled (licence, see
  `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` M26-02); the mapping is not validated against the official schema.
* Test: `tests/integration/test_m26_letting.py` (incl. `test_listing_openimmo_export`).
* Open: M26-01, M26-02 (XSD validation), M26-03, M26-04, A-036.

## A63 Energieausweis und Angebotsmiete (26.09.2026)

* `Property` (migration 0112): `energy_certificate_type` (verbrauch, bedarf),
  `energy_certificate_value` (kWh/(m²a), NUMERIC(8,2)), `energy_certificate_source`,
  `energy_certificate_construction_year`, `energy_certificate_issued_on`,
  `energy_certificate_valid_until`, `energy_certificate_class`; validity before the issue
  date is rejected. `Listing`: `energy_issued_on`, `energy_building_year` added to the existing
  energy fields; asking rent stays on the listing (`price`, `additional_costs`,
  `heating_costs`, `deposit`). `listing.energy_status` widened to String(24)
  ("nicht_erforderlich" has 18 characters, the column was String(16)).
* Listing creation copies the property's certificate (`energy_status` becomes `liegt_vor`)
  unless the caller sets a status; the values stay editable per listing.
* Exposé (`GET /letting/units/{unit_id}/expose`): blocks `energy_certificate` (property) and
  `asking_rent` (newest rental listing, `listing_id`) with per field `missing` entries
  (`energy_certificate.type`, `asking_rent.net_rent`, ...) instead of the free text markers.
* OpenImmo (`mhvp.letting.openimmo`): `additional_costs` required for rentals, hints for
  heating costs, deposit, energy source, issue date and building year; export writes
  `preise/heizkosten`, `preise/heizkosten_enthalten`, `energiepass/baujahr` and
  `energiepass/ausstelldatum` (not validated against the official XSD, M26-02).
* CRM: energy certificate form on the property page
  (`components/properties/EnergyCertificateForm.tsx`, PUT with If-Match), issue date and
  building year on listing create and detail, exposé page shows both blocks.
* Tests: `test_m26_letting.py::test_energy_certificate_and_asking_rent` plus extended export
  and exposé assertions; `EnergyCertificateForm.test.tsx`, `ListingDetail.test.tsx`.
* Open: M26-03 (which values are legally mandatory in an advertisement) stays with the
  operator and legal counsel; the check remains Produktschutz.
