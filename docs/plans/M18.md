# M18 Auswertungen und Exporte: plan and result

Status: evaluations and neutral journal export implemented; DATEV and GoBD formats open
(2026-09-23). Source: MASTER-PROMPT 7.5, 7.7, 18 (M18).
Acceptance: tax advisor imports the DATEV batch (open, M18-01); GoBD export complete (open).

## Decisions

* `mhvp.accounting.reports`, migration 0018 (export_run): liquidity forecast 90 days per
  ledger with free funds, reserves, segregated deposits and expected in- and outflows apart
  (receivables are not liquidity); payments by debtor; revenue per revenue account; open
  items as of a date (M10); journal CSV with SHA-256 and export record.
* Tax advisor role: read and export accounting, no posting.
* DATEV endpoint refuses until the format data is specified.
* 25.09.2026 (M18-01, docs/rules/M18-03.md): the DATEV parameters (consultant number, client
  number, chart of accounts SKR03/SKR04, account length, fiscal year start month) moved from a
  fixed refusal to per tenant fields on `TenantBillingSettings` (migration 0069, shared with
  M13-04). `mhvp.accounting.reports.datev_csv` emits a DATEV EXTF Buchungsstapel header (format
  version 7, category 21) once all three required fields are set; CRM account numbers are
  emitted unmapped and the `ExportRun.note` records "Kontenzuordnung zu prüfen". The endpoint
  still refuses with MHVP-BILL-0004 otherwise.
