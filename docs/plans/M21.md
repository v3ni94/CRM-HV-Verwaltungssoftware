### Nachtrag 26.09.2026 (A55, Foto als Dokumentverknüpfung)

* `POST /api/v1/portal/tickets` nimmt `document_ids` (bis zu zehn) entgegen. Zulässig sind nur
  Dokumente, die dasselbe Portalkonto zuvor über `POST /portal/uploads` angelegt hat
  (`Document.created_by` und `source = portal`); fremde, CRM seitige oder unbekannte IDs werden
  einheitlich mit 404 beantwortet (`mhvp.portal.routers._own_uploads`), damit das Portal nicht
  erfährt, ob eine fremde ID existiert. Verknüpfung als `DocumentLink` (`entity_type="ticket"`,
  Rolle `attachment`); die Anhänge erscheinen in `GET /portal/tickets` und im CRM Ticket-Detail.
* Portal-Upload bereinigt Fotos wie Übergabefotos (M30-04): `mhvp.handover.images.sanitize_image`
  entfernt EXIF, GPS, XMP, ICC und PNG-Textchunks, dreht nach EXIF-Orientierung und skaliert auf
  `handover_image_max_edge`; TIFF wird ebenfalls neu kodiert, HEIC wird abgelehnt (Hinweis JPEG
  oder PNG), weil der Sanitizer es nicht neu kodieren kann und ein Foto nie mit Aufnahmedaten
  gespeichert werden soll. Das Original wird nicht aufbewahrt.
* `NewTicket.tsx` lädt mehrere Fotos vorab hoch und übergibt die IDs; der frühere Hinweis in der
  Beschreibung entfällt. Ticket-Detail zeigt die Anhänge.
* Tests: `tests/integration/test_a55_a58_portal_attachments.py` (Verknüpfung, fremdes und CRM
  Dokument 404, Metadaten entfernt, Mandantentrennung), `tests/unit/test_m30_handover_images.py`
  (TIFF, `supports`), `NewTicket.test.tsx` (Upload vor Anlage, Abbruch bei Upload-Fehler).

## Nachtrag 26.09.2026: Portal Eigentümer, lesend (A51, section 14 role owner)

* `mhvp/portal/owner.py` (router registered in `main.py`): `GET /api/v1/portal/resolutions`,
  `GET /api/v1/portal/property-contacts`, `GET /api/v1/portal/hoa-account`. Every endpoint
  requires an active grant with legal basis `hoa_member_right` (role owner); tenants, providers
  and staff accounts get 403. Scope comes from the grants only (`_owner_scope`), RLS via
  `tenant_tx`. No gate is opened: nothing is written, no statement is produced.
* Resolutions: rows of `hoa.Resolution` of the own communities, newest first. A row exists only
  once a result was announced (`/hoa/agenda/{id}/announce`), a circular resolution was recorded
  or an external resolution was captured; a tally without announcement is a proposal and never
  listed. Output: number, date, subject, wording, status, kind, majority basis and a vote
  summary (yes, no, abstain) from the announced tally or the text form consents.
* Property contacts: per property of the own ownership contracts the manager display name
  (`Property.manager_user_id`) and the `PropertyContact` rows of the categories `caretaker` and
  `emergency` that carry `owner` in `visible_in_portal_for` and are valid today. Phone numbers
  with label `private` are never returned; no e-mail, no user ids.
* Hoa fee account: posted journal lines (`EntryStatus.POSTED`) on the owner's debtor account
  (via `DebtorAccountReservation.number`) in the ledger of the community. Debits are charges,
  credits are payments or credit notes, balance = charges minus credits; drafts never appear,
  reversed entries are flagged. Without a ledger the contract is listed with the note
  `NO_LEDGER_NOTE` and no amounts. The response always carries the note "keine Abrechnung,
  keine Rechtsfolge" and, while the ledger's leading system is the legacy system, the same
  provisional note as `/portal/account`.
* Portal UI: `/beschluesse`, `/ansprechpartner`, `/hausgeldkonto` (server pages via
  `serverFetch`, 403 shows an owners only notice), components `ResolutionList`,
  `PropertyContactList`, `HoaAccountTable` (amounts `1.234,56 EUR`, dates `TT.MM.JJJJ`);
  navigation and start tiles only with role `owner`; BFF allowlist extended for the three GET
  paths; messages `Resolutions`, `Contacts`, `HoaAccount` in de and en.
* Tests: `tests/integration/test_m21_portal_owner.py` (announced only, contacts without private
  data, posted lines and balance, tenant 403, foreign tenant sees nothing, no CRM right),
  `apps/web-portal/src/components/portal/OwnerPages.test.tsx`, `StartTiles.test.tsx` (owner
  tiles). Open: board members and utilities are not listed as contacts (M21-03 board role);
  the owner sees the manager's display name only, a central phone number of the management
  is not part of the master data (decision operator).

## Nachtrag 26.09.2026: Lesebestätigung im CRM (A53, D34) und Schwarzes Brett (A54)

* A53 (11.3, D34): the read receipt endpoint of a document is
  `GET /api/v1/documents/{id}/portal-read-receipts` (permission `documents:read`, tenant
  separation by RLS; the published path `/read-receipts` stays as an alias with the same
  answer, a deprecation mark follows with ADR 0009). It returns the
  retrievals per portal account with time and kind (`opened`, `downloaded`) and the note of
  `mhvp.portal.read_receipts.LEGAL_NOTE`. CRM page `apps/web-crm/src/app/(app)/dokumente/[documentId]`
  (document reads stay outside the BFF allowlist, therefore read server side) with component
  `components/documents/PortalReadReceipts.tsx` and the fixed wording "Indiz, keine Zustellung,
  keine Rechtsfolge". Test D34: `tests/integration/test_m21_read_receipts.py`
  (`test_d34_read_receipt_is_an_indication_apart_from_delivery`: list writes nothing, open and
  download are recorded with time, account and kind, no dispatch row, document unchanged,
  portal user gets 403 on the CRM endpoint; `test_d34_expired_invitation_triggers_no_legal_consequence`:
  expired invitation is refused with 422, account stays `invited`, no receipt, no dispatch,
  no event beyond `portal_account.invited`, document unchanged).
* A54 (14, M21-01): `mhvp/portal/notice_routers.py`. CRM: `GET/POST /api/v1/properties/{id}/notices`,
  `PATCH /api/v1/notices/{id}`, `POST /api/v1/notices/{id}/end` (writes with `properties:update`,
  the list with `properties:read`; the task named `properties:write`, which does not exist in the
  permission catalogue). Fields: title, text, valid from/to, audience `tenant|owner|all`, optional

  `GET /api/v1/portal/notices` (properties of the active grants via unit and GdWE scope, matching

  read receipt). UI: `components/properties/PropertyNotices.tsx` on the object page,
  portal page `/aushaenge` with `NoticeList.tsx`, navigation link, tile and a hint on new
  notices on the start page (`StartTiles.tsx`). BFF allowlists of both apps extended.
* Tests: `tests/integration/test_m21_notices.py` (validation, maintenance, end, visibility per
  role and property, validity incl. last valid day, attachment visibility, no read receipt,
  portal user without CRM right, second tenant reaches nothing), Vitest
